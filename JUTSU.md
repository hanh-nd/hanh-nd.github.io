# Jutsu, FLOW, 2v2

## Chakra and arming
- Chakra is the ring around each name in the scorebug, cut into **3 seals**; it glows when full. Next to each name, **3 seal orbs** light up one per seal, in the colour of the jutsu that level fires, and the ring takes the colour of the jutsu ready now. When you reach a new level, **"Rasengan ready"** (or whichever) pops up once with a chime.
- The ring has **3 seals**. Clean returns, long rallies and every point (won or lost) fill it. A counter refunds a lot.
- **Arm:** **double-tap** the table, tap your row in the scorebug, touch with a **second finger**, press **Space**, or right-click. It arms the **biggest jutsu your seals pay for**. Do it again to cancel.
- **Pick a smaller one:** while armed, tap a jutsu in the list under the scorebug, or press **1**, **2** or **3**. Tapping the lit one cancels too.
- **Any stroke fires it:** block, push, drive or smash. The stroke no longer picks the jutsu.
- **Auto jutsu** (Settings, for one hand): when all 3 seals are full, your next stroke fires jutsu 3 without a tap.

| Kit slot | Cost |
|---|---|
| Jutsu 1 | ● |
| Jutsu 2 | ●● |
| Jutsu 3 | ●●● |

- Each side fires at most one jutsu per point. A jutsu is chakra-guided, so it has no hand error.

CPU kits by style: All-rounder (Shadow Clone · Boomerang Snake · Rasengan), Blocker (Copy Wheel · Yata Mirror · God Hand), Defender (Meta Vision · Time Stop · Infinite Void), Spin Wizard (Zero-Shiki · Misdirection · Awakening), Attacker (Samehada · Shadow Possession · Rasengan). Black Flash and Flying Thunder God are yours only. When a CPU fires, a call-out names the jutsu and its counter, and the CPU's racket glows. A smart CPU (its level's iq: Easy 0.25, Medium 0.55, Hard 0.85) saves up for its bigger jutsu, fires and goes big on a game point, and doesn't waste a jutsu that would do nothing (Samehada against an empty bar, a power shot into a mirror it read).

### Reads and combos (Nov 2026)
Jutsu now interact, and the strongest plays are reads:
- **Bait the smash:** arm Yata Mirror. Your ball floats up, and if they take the bait and smash, it comes back faster than they hit it. If they read it and play soft, the mirror stays up for the rest of the point: no hard ball at you until it ends.
- **Possession → smash:** Shadow Possession makes their next ball float to you. Smash it. Against a CPU Possession, keep a seal: a ● jutsu on the bound stroke breaks it (the jutsu itself is wasted).
- **Possession vs Mirror:** bound, fire Yata Mirror. It's the one jutsu that both breaks the shadow and works: your stroke becomes the bait for the smash they were setting up.
- **Copy Wheel** copies Possession and Mirror too.
- **Samehada is a forcing move:** fire it and they must spend now (or lose 2 seals if you win the point). Spamming it no longer locks the CPU out. In the sim it used to cut the CPU's jutsu from 0.21 to 0.02 per point; now it stays at 0.20.

### Balance (Nov 2026: every seal worth about the same)
Before, the cheap jutsu were the best deal: the ●●● jutsu won the point barely more often than the ● ones at three times the cost, so spamming ● beat saving up, and a CPU that saved up lost more points.

Now the bigger the jutsu, the more surely it wins the point. With the scripted bot firing one tier whenever it can vs Medium (`sim/jutsu.sh`), here is how often the bot won the points each jutsu was fired in. The same stroke with no effect (a Black Flash fizzle) wins about 0.55.

| Cost | Jutsu |
|---|---|
| ● | Shadow Clone 0.67, Zero-Shiki 0.66, Meta Vision 0.61, Samehada 0.54 (it pays later, in seals) |
| ●● | Black Flash 0.84 (when it lands), Misdirection 0.84, Boomerang Snake 0.83, Time Stop 0.81, Shadow Possession 0.77, Flying Thunder God 0.74, Yata Mirror 0.73 |
| ●●● | God Hand 0.92, Infinite Void 0.88, Rasengan 0.84, Awakening 0.81 (plus its FLOW in later points) |

That's about +0.1 to +0.15 points per seal whichever you pick. In CPU-vs-CPU duels a CPU that saves up for its big jutsu now does as well as one that spends at once (0.50 of points), so a CPU that saves up no longer gives points away. The CPU's own jutsu against you are about as strong as before: you win 0.48 / 0.39 of points vs Medium / Hard (0.46 / 0.37 before).

## Your jutsu
- You always play as yourself. **Your jutsu** is one row of 3 chips (●, ●●, ●●●), shown on the **Ready** screen before every Practice, 1v1 and 2v2 match, and in the tournament hub under your next opponent. Each chip shows its angle. Tap a chip to see what it does and the other jutsu of that cost, then tap one to swap it in.
- Your pick is saved and is used in every mode: Practice, 1v1, 2v2 and the tournament.

## Menus
- **Home** asks one thing: the mode (**Practice**, **1v1**, **2v2**, **Tournament**). ⚙ Settings and ? How to play are in its corner. **Play again** replays your last Practice, 1v1 or 2v2 setup in one tap.
- **Ready** comes straight after the mode, filled in with last time's choices: who you face, the level, your jutsu, Start. To change who's in, tap their card (it says **Change ▾**) or their side of the VS banner: the roster opens in a sheet, grouped by style (★ = a signature shot), and one tap picks and closes it.
- **1v1 and Practice:** the rival's card shows their style, top jutsu and signature counter. The roster sheet also has **Random** (a new rival each time you reach Ready). **Change Rival** at the end of a 1v1 opens Ready with the roster up.
- **2v2:** three roster players: **your ally** and **Opponent 1 and 2**, each a card to tap. Picking someone already in swaps them; **Re-roll** draws two new opponents of different styles. Your ally is remembered.
- **Tournament:** choose an event, then the hub. With a run in progress, the tile says who's next and opens the hub; **New Event** in the hub starts over.
- **Practice:** a 1v1 that never ends (games roll on, no rank change, no grade). Two switches on Ready: **Seals refill every point** (on) and **CPU uses jutsu**. Leave from the pause menu. A tag under the scorebug names each combo as you land it (gold on the rival's weak spot), or warns **Careful · it read you**.
- **Game speed** is in Settings and applies straight away. Every choice (rival, 2v2 ally, level, event, speed, practice switches) is remembered.

## Venues
- Each kind of match has its own venue, all built in code (no image files). The menus show the venue of what you are choosing (pick an event to preview its venue).
  - **School gym** (Club Open, Practice): wooden floor with court lines, bleachers, hoops, tube lights, the club's sign, a wall scoreboard.
  - **Arena** (1v1, 2v2, Pro Tour): seats on all four sides, an upper tier behind an LED ribbon, floodlights, big screens with the live score.
  - **Stadium** (World Championship): a bigger arena, packed, with the nations' flags hanging from the roof.
- **Fans** sit on seats, stand and jump to cheer, raise their arms or clap, and follow the ball with their heads. Your fans wear your colour and the rival's fans wear theirs; some wave flags, and the banners on the stands name the players. Phone flashes go off after big points. Attendance grows round by round in a tournament.
- Around the court: a coach for each player (they cheer their player's points) and towel boxes.

## FLOW, Ego rank
- **FLOW:** 3 points in a row. Until you lose a point: hand error ×0.6 (steadier aim) and chakra gain ×1.6. It does not slow time (that is Meta Vision). A CPU in FLOW makes 40% fewer mistakes. Awakening's **deep FLOW** is stronger (hand error ×0.4, a CPU 65% fewer mistakes, reach ×1.3) and survives one lost point.
- **Ego rank:** starts at #300, climbs with wins (more against Hard, ×1.2 in 2v2, ×1.5 in the tournament), slips 4 on a loss. Each match ends with an S–D grade.

## 2v2
- You + an ally vs 2 opponents, all from the roster at the level you pick. Partners **alternate hits**; the racket whose turn it isn't fades out and can't touch the ball.
- The serve goes **diagonally**, right half to right half (the target half glows). It rotates A1→B1→A2→B2.
- The team shares one chakra bar: your ally fills it, and only you spend it. The CPU pair shares theirs too; whichever of them is hitting fires from its own style's kit. One jutsu per team per point.
- **Signatures** are on for all three (a player who owns one: ★). They cost no chakra, so your ally can play its signature even though it never fires a jutsu; it's announced as your ally's, with no counter.
