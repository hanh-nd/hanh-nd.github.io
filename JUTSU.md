# Jutsu, FLOW, 2v2

## Chakra and arming
- The bar has **3 seals**. Clean returns, long rallies and every point (won or lost) fill it. A counter refunds a lot.
- **Arm:** **double-tap** the table, tap your row in the scorebug, touch with a **second finger**, press **Space**, or right-click. It arms the **biggest jutsu your seals pay for**. Do it again to cancel.
- **Pick a smaller one:** while armed, tap a jutsu in the list under the scorebug, or press **1**, **2** or **3**. Tapping the lit one cancels too.
- **Any stroke fires it:** block, push, drive or smash. The stroke no longer picks the jutsu.
- **Auto jutsu** (Settings, for one hand): when all 3 seals are full, your next stroke fires jutsu 3 without a tap.

- Each side fires at most one jutsu per point. A jutsu is chakra-guided, so it has no hand error.

When a CPU fires, a call-out names the jutsu and its counter, and the CPU's racket glows.

## Your jutsu
- You always play as yourself. The start menu shows **Your jutsu**: 3 cards (●, ●●, ●●●). Tap **Swap** on one to see the other 4 jutsu of that cost, then tap one to take it.
- Your pick is saved and is used in every mode: 1v1, 2v2 and the tournament.

## FLOW, Ego rank
- **FLOW:** 3 points in a row. Until you lose a point: hand error ×0.6 (steadier aim) and chakra gain ×1.6. It does not slow time (that is Meta Vision). A CPU in FLOW makes 40% fewer mistakes.
- **Ego rank:** starts at #300, climbs with wins (more against Hard, ×1.2 in 2v2, ×1.5 in the tournament), slips 4 on a loss. Each match ends with an S–D grade.

## 2v2
- You + a CPU ally vs 2 CPUs. Partners **alternate hits**; the racket whose turn it isn't fades out and can't touch the ball.
- The serve goes **diagonally**, right half to right half (the target half glows). It rotates A1→B1→A2→B2.
- The team shares one chakra bar: your ally fills it, and only you spend it.
