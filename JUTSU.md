# Jutsu, FLOW, 2v2

## Chakra and arming
- The bar has **3 seals**. Clean returns, long rallies and every point (won or lost) fill it. A counter refunds a lot.
- **Arm:** tap **印** (bottom-left), touch with a **second finger**, press **Space**, or right-click. Arm again to cancel.
- **Auto jutsu** (Settings, for one hand): when all 3 seals are full, your next stroke fires its jutsu without a tap.
- The stroke you play picks the jutsu from your player's kit:

| Stroke | Kit slot | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Block / push | Jutsu 1 | ● |
| Drive | Jutsu 2 | ●● |
| Smash | Jutsu 3 | ●●● |

- Each side fires at most one jutsu per point. A jutsu is chakra-guided, so it has no hand error.

The CPU's kit depends on its style: All-rounder (Clone · Rasenshuriken · Rasengan), Blocker (Sharingan · Chidori · Amaterasu), Defender (Shield · Coffin · Tsunami), Spin Wizard (Meta Vision · Direct · Awakening), Attacker (Feint · Dribble · Monster). When a CPU fires, a call-out names the jutsu and its counter, and the CPU's racket glows.

## You play as (roster)
- 8 fictional players. Each has the same stat budget (45), spread differently, plus a 3-jutsu kit.
- **Stats:**
  - **Power:** stroke pace
  - **Spin:** topspin
  - **Speed:** footwork
  - **Control:** hand error
  - **Reach:** hit radius and arm
  - **Chakra:** gain
  - **Mental:** steadier at deuce or game point; 8+ reaches FLOW one point sooner
- Kenji, Shen and Wang are open from the start. The others unlock as your Ego rank climbs (#280, #250, #200, #150, #100).

## FLOW, Ego rank
- **FLOW:** 3 points in a row (2 with high Mental). You aim steadier and gain chakra faster until you lose a point. The CPU can flow too.
- **Ego rank:** starts at #300, climbs with wins (more against Hard, ×1.2 in 2v2, ×1.5 in the tournament), slips 4 on a loss. Each match ends with an S–D grade.

## 2v2
- You + a CPU ally vs 2 CPUs. Partners **alternate hits**; the racket whose turn it isn't fades out and can't touch the ball.
- The serve goes **diagonally**, right half to right half (the target half glows). It rotates A1→B1→A2→B2.
- The team shares one chakra bar: your ally fills it, and only you spend it.
