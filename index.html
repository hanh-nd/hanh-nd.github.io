Table tennis. Games to 11, win by 2, best of 3.

Game speed
Move: Mouse / drag anywhere / WASD. Your racket can only reach so far from where you stand.
Strokes — how you swipe as the ball arrives:
 · hold still = block · swipe forward = drive (faster = harder, more topspin)
 · quick flick forward (or Shift) = smash — safe on a ball that sits up high; a low, fast or spinny ball often goes long or into the net. A gentler flick is safer
 · pull back = push (backspin) · hit off-center or swipe sideways to angle the ball
Read the spin from the CPU's racket face and the ball's dots: topspin → block or drive; backspin (a chop) → push. The wrong stroke drifts long or short — a hard drive can fly out.
Serve: hold Space / mouse / finger, release to toss (red zone = long). Swipe as the ball falls: forward = topspin, pull back = backspin.
Q/E or Wheel tilt the face: backspin / topspin · P/Esc/❚❚ pause · H bounce marker · M mute
Rules: let the ball bounce on your side first — touching it before is a volley and loses the point. Edge balls count; the side of the table doesn't.
