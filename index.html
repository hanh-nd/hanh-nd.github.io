Tournament rules
  • Single elimination, 16 players. Lose a match and you're out.
  • Round of 16, Quarterfinal & Semifinal: best of 3 games. Final: best of 5.
  • Games to 11, win by 2. Serve passes every 2 points — every point from 10–10.
  • The first server alternates each game. A serve that clips the net and lands in is a let: served again.
  • The serve must bounce on your side, then theirs. In a rally the ball must bounce on your side before you hit it — no volleys.
  • Edge balls count; the side of the table doesn't. Leaving a match unfinished doesn't count — you can replay it.
  • ▶ Watch any other match of the round before it's decided — the result you watch counts. Knocked out? Keep watching to the final.

How to play

Move: Mouse / drag anywhere. Your racket can only reach so far from where you stand.
Strokes — how you swipe as the ball arrives:
 · hold still = block · swipe forward = drive (faster = harder, more topspin)
 · quick flick forward = smash — safe on a ball that sits up high; smashing a ball near net height is a gamble (often long or into the net), so push or drive those. A gentler flick is safer
 · pull back = push (backspin) — pull back harder for a long push · hit off-center or swipe sideways to angle the ball toward a corner
 · firm flick sideways (left → right or right → left) = sidespin — a brush, not a push: the ball comes back soft, drifts a little along your flick and curves back the other way, and the spin kicks the CPU's return toward the edge. A slow sideways drag is just a normal stroke
 · short touch: step in and meet a slow ball over the table, holding still — it drops just over the net and bounces twice. The CPU can only push it back or flick it — some love to flick
Scout your opponents: every style plays two patterns (a racket wind-up warns of the key shot), headline players have a signature shot — the racket glows, a chime plays, and the wrong stroke against it dies in the net or flies long. Lose a point to one and you're told the counter.
Read the spin from the CPU's racket face and the ball's dots: topspin → block or drive; backspin (a chop) → push. The wrong stroke drifts long or short — a hard drive can fly out.
Serve: hold mouse / finger, release to toss (red zone = long). Swipe as the ball falls: forward = topspin, pull back = backspin.
Wheel tilts the face: backspin / topspin · on-screen buttons: ❚❚ pause, ⚙ settings (sound, touch sensitivity)
Rules: let the ball bounce on your side first — touching it before is a volley and loses the point. Edge balls count; the side of the table doesn't.
